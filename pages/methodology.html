Methodology

How entries are labeled, sourced, and updated.

Home Disclaimer Contact / Corrections

Labels

  • Alleged: credible allegations exist (e.g., lawsuits, regulator complaints, investigative reporting). Not proven.
  • Charged: criminal charges filed by a government authority.
  • Convicted: a conviction or guilty plea is recorded.
  • Sanctioned: official sanctions/enforcement actions (e.g., OFAC, SEC/CFTC enforcement, etc.).
  • Settled: settlement/consent order without trial (may or may not admit wrongdoing).

Sources

  • Prefer primary documents: court filings, indictments, judgments, regulator press releases, official dockets.
  • Secondary reporting should be reputable and should link to primary documents where possible.
  • Each timeline item should include at least one citation.

What we do NOT publish

  • Doxxing: personal addresses, personal phone numbers, or non-public personal info.
  • Unverifiable claims (anonymous screenshots without corroboration, rumors, etc.).
  • Calls to harassment.

Corrections

We provide a corrections channel. If you are listed and believe information is incorrect, contact us with supporting documentation.
